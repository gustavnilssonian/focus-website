Frequently asked questions

The phones do not find each other during co-observation

Check that Bluetooth is on for all phones and that FOCUS is allowed to use Bluetooth (Settings → FOCUS on iPhone). Keep the phones close together and check that the code is entered correctly. If it still does not work, close the app on all phones and start again.

How do I send data to a shared spreadsheet?

See For observers. In short: Settings → Google Sheets, create a sheet or connect to one shared with you.

Google warns that the app is not verified

While the app is being reviewed by Google, invited test users see this warning. It disappears once the review is complete.

The export file is not attached to the email

Use the Send by email button in the export sheet – the file is then always attached. The Share option goes through the phone's share menu, where not all email apps include attachments.

How do I delete data?

Delete an observation under earlier observations by swiping it to the left. Uninstalling the app removes all observations from the phone. Rows in a shared spreadsheet are deleted in the spreadsheet.

How do I remove the app's access to my Google account?

In the app: Settings → Google Sheets → Disconnect and Sign out of Google. Access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

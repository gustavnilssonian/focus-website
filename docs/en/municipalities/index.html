A shared spreadsheet for the municipality's observers

Observers in a municipality can collect their observations in a shared Google spreadsheet. When an observation is finished, its summary is sent there automatically – one row per observation. The municipality owns the spreadsheet and decides who can see it.

Getting started

  1. One person creates the spreadsheet. In FOCUS: Settings → Google Sheets → Create a new sheet. Sign in with the municipality's Google account.
  2. Share the spreadsheet with your colleagues as Editors, using the Share button in Google Sheets.
  3. Colleagues connect. In FOCUS: Settings → Google Sheets → Connect to an existing sheet. They scan the QR code – it is in the spreadsheet's Instructions tab and under Invite observers in the app – or paste the sheet's address.
  4. Done. Each observation is sent when it is finished. Without a connection it waits on the phone and is sent later. If the same observation is sent again, its row is updated rather than duplicated.

What the spreadsheet contains

The spreadsheet has three tabs:

  • Instructions – instructions and a QR code for connecting.
  • Summary – one row per observation: time, series ID, municipality, observer, school, subject, grade, class, number of students, notes, measurement point and all results. For co-observations also the agreement between observers.
  • Codebook – what each column means and how it is calculated.
The teacher's name is never sent. The series ID links baseline and follow-up of the same teacher and class without names.

Good to know

  • Do not change the column headers in the Summary tab – the app finds the columns by their names. Your own tabs, charts and columns at the far right are fine.
  • New versions of the app may add columns. They are always added at the end, so existing formulas and charts keep working.
  • Each observer needs a Google account. It can be created with a work email address; it does not have to be Gmail.
  • If the municipality uses Google Workspace, the administrator may need to approve FOCUS before observers can sign in.
  • The municipality is the data controller for the content of the spreadsheet.
